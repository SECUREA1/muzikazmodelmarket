Deed utility

Build, publish, host

A canonical land deed unlocks avatar, asset, game, and environment upload gates. Owner and delegated roles control editing and placement; visitors retain public exploration and play. A deed is a world permission object, not merely a map picture.

Atlas design

Connected places

Volt City, Skyline Deck, Echo Gardens, Crew Plaza, Studio Ridge, Neon Docks, Bassline Badlands, and Pixel Peaks form the fixed route. Public-area claims are deeds inside those areas; dynamic wild-land claims may add one owner pin without inventing a fixed global supply.

Canonical-state rule: browser storage is only a display cache. Land ownership, MZK debits, deed assignment, listings, and world memory resolve through persistent wallet records. A permanent world object is valid only when profile, Backpack, space, map pixel, and owned asset agree.

Physical expression connects back to the character.

MUZIKAZ’s retail layer turns world identity into products people can wear, collect, customize, scan, and bring back into digital experiences. Product checkout and digital ownership are distinct records; a physical order does not imply token ownership unless a drop explicitly grants it.

04 · MZK economy

Every intake and burn leaves a receipt.

MZK is the platform utility and accounting unit used to access functions—not equity, debt, a share of revenue, or a promise of appreciation. The current store quote uses 100 MZK per US dollar, with a $40 / 4,000 MZK land-purchase minimum. Quotes, bonuses, and availability remain offer terms, not market-value guarantees.

Intake

Value enters only after proof.

External-payment intake remains pending until network verification. Reward intake requires a unique event ID. Loadout codes are one-time credentials: activation consumes the code and materializes its assets without silently replacing existing wallet state.

Debit

Authorization precedes function.

The engine checks identity, balance, ownership, price, and request ID before changing both asset and MZK state. Duplicate requests resolve to the original result. A failed operation must not debit the wallet.

Burn

Retirement must be explicit.

A spend is not automatically a burn. Transfers credit a seller; refundable reservations remain liabilities; treasury intake remains circulating until separately retired. Only an irreversible, publicly classified retirement may be reported as burned.

{
  "eventId": "mzk:settlement:<unique-request-id>",
  "walletId": "<authenticated-wallet>",
  "assetId": "<land-product-or-consumable>",
  "amountMzk": 5,
  "function": "consumable_refill",
  "disposition": "burn",
  "status": "settled",
  "createdAt": "<ISO-8601>"
}

Transparency commitment: Until a server-backed burn registry and supply counters are published, the interface must call implemented reductions spends or debits, not token burns. Future burn reporting should expose gross intake, transfers, treasury-held units, reversed spends, retired units, and net circulating change without exposing private user data.

Function metadata: each asset should declare controller, type, media, compatibility, allowed actions, world scope, transferability, consumability, quantity, cost, cooldown, expiration, approval state, and provenance. Interfaces must disable unsupported functions rather than imply them.

Backpack references, never duplicates

An inventory entry records the asset ID, acquisition state, and listing lock. The canonical service resolves current media, traits, and compatibility. Transfers change the controller without moving large model data.

Active avatar is a session choice

Before spawn, a trusted service authenticates the player; authorizes control and world access; checks approval status; resolves pinned media; validates the loadout; calculates effective stats; and signs a short-lived, non-transferable session snapshot.

09 · Interoperability

A strict creator standard makes an open world possible.

Asset validation

glTF 2.0 / GLB, optional USDZ, declared byte and geometry budgets, PBR textures, HTTPS or same-origin URLs, collision bounds, units in meters, origin, scale, and spawn clearance.

Rig validation

Versioned rig profile, attachment bones, skin weights, humanoid or custom classification, neutral pose, root motion policy, and retargeting map.

Animation contract

Supported semantic action names include: idle, walk, run, jump, turn, interact, dance, emote, use_tool, damage, and defeat.

Trust and rights

Creator identity, rights declaration, malware and unsafe-content scans, moderation state, SHA-256 content digest, version history, and a revocation path for compromised files.
