01 · State of the game

A playable platform, with a growing creator toolkit.

The current product combines browser-based 3D exploration with server-managed rooms, a scene editor and an account inventory. It supports authored environments and procedural builds. Gameplay runs through specific activities and configured objects; the platform is not yet a single, finished campaign spanning every map.

03 · Worlds, districts and land

A shared directory, with distinct places inside it.

The atlas is a navigation system for multiple maps and rooms. Connected destinations share identity, inventory and routing services; entering another destination changes the active room. They are not all simulated as one uninterrupted terrain or one unlimited multiplayer match.

Full world maps

Large procedural layouts, district generators and compatible 3D environments provide the surrounding space. Builder world-size choices extend up to 320 metres for supported procedural presets.

Activity scenes

Fourteen compact sets—including the firing range, vendor street, mazes, arenas and movie studio—can be placed inside a world and moved, rotated and scaled independently.

Rooms & objects

Eight small inlay choices provide floors, rooms and compounds. Buildings, furnishings, machines, characters and other items retain their own placement and gameplay configuration.

Hosted destinations

Published maps and revealed deed entitlements resolve to stable server destinations. Transfers change the owner of an eligible deed while keeping its hosted room and saved content.

Public world

Districts and commons

The original atlas seed lists eight district pins, five public-area plots and seven connecting routes. Those seed figures are not the total number of playable destinations: published builds, repository environments and hosted land extend the directory.

Land entitlement

Ownership and access

A deed is an account entitlement with a hosted location and applicable build or upload permissions. Public exploration, free built-in placement, paid asset ownership and permission to publish or modify a destination are separate decisions.

Destination integrity: a pin or portal points to a server map record. Room access and current ownership are checked by the relevant service. Missing assets, private destinations or an unavailable backend can prevent entry; the roadmap calls for clearer diagnostics and broader destination coverage tests.

04 · The world builder

A world, its scenes and its objects. Independently editable.

The creator dashboard groups tools by task, with category browsing, searchable controls and paged panels on desktop and mobile. Building is a composition workflow: choose the surrounding map, place smaller experiences inside it, then furnish and configure them.

  1. Choose a worldMap, size, weather and base alignment
  2. Add scenesRooms, activity sets and camp stacks
  3. Place assetsTap a destination or drag onto the map
  4. ConfigureTransforms, appearance and interactions
  5. Test & publishSave through the server and open the returned link
Separate map and scene controls

Move the activity, keep the world.

In-map activity scenes have their own placement list and sliders for X, Z, floor offset, rotation and scale. A firing range can sit off-center inside a district. Its geometry and collision move with the module; generating a new world preserves placed activity modules and independent inlays.

Object tuning

Fine adjustments stay available.

Placed items support position, elevation, rotation, scale, duplicate, remove, focus and existing undo controls. World alignment and the placed-object layer can also be adjusted separately. Editor visibility toggles aid composition; hiding a layer in the editor does not remove it from saved gameplay.

The current creation toolkit

Buildings & interiors

Twenty-five enterable building templates cover taverns, saloons, shops, banks, services and social venues. Furniture and interactive devices can be placed inside suitable interiors.

Drawing & depth

Custom item tools preserve vector strokes, colors, depth, bevel, material and configured behavior. Generated forms provide playable geometry; an extruded drawing is not automatically a rigged character.

Appearance & motion

Avatar Look Lab, compatible fitted pieces, material accents and available animation clips support customization. Each scene instance retains its own saved settings.

Gameplay authoring

Objects can be configured as decoration, seats, vehicles, conversations, quests, enemies, pickups, doors or supported devices. An object’s appearance alone does not create working gameplay.

Camp maps combine open arrival lanes, cabins, tents, gathering areas, trail collectibles and a shared arcade. Each preset remains editable after generation.

Small experiences inside a map

Add a campfire circle, cabin retreat or adventure trail without replacing the world. Camp benches offer seating; the trail quest grants 35 room score after collecting its required items and returning.

Camp activity scope: the canoe rack, lake and archery target are scenery or props. They do not claim a swimming, canoe-driving or archery game. The implemented camp activities are configured collection quests, seating, climbing and the existing shared arcade.

Save draft, Test and Publish have different effects.

Save draft keeps editable data in this browser. Both Test and Publish send the scene to the custom-map service after the applicable guest/member access checks; Test then opens its play link. A successful server response establishes the saved destination. Testing should not be described as a guaranteed private, browser-only session. Built-in sandbox placement does not grant ownership of paid models or unrestricted downloads.

05 · Gameplay and multiplayer

Shared rules on the server. Responsive presentation in the browser.

Shared room state

Simulation and authority

  • Player identity, room entry and supported movement commands
  • Collision, interaction distance, ownership and cooldown checks
  • Supported combat, pickups, seats, quests and vehicle actions
  • Machine controls, arcade turns and shared playback state
Client presentation

Controls and feedback

  • 3D rendering, cameras, animation and sound output
  • Keyboard/mouse, touch joysticks and on-screen actions
  • Movement smoothing and received-state interpolation
  • Compatible WebXR headset input and AR previews

RAD-TOX supplies a combat encounter loop in eligible environments; builder maps use their authored objects and activities. Room-level quest completion, health, scores and equipped or held state belong to gameplay data rather than an immutable avatar definition. Features can vary between hosted multiplayer, solo play and custom maps.

Placement geometry supports walkable floors, solid obstacles and gradual steps. Floor following, collision handling and reconciliation aim to keep walking consistent as shared state arrives. The project still needs repeatable performance measurements across physical phones, desktop GPUs and increasing room sizes before publishing frame-rate or capacity targets.

Progression scope: character classes and traits describe roles such as creator, builder, explorer, fighter, companion and merchant. A universal XP campaign, fully balanced competitive modes and identical progression across every custom game are development goals, not established platform-wide guarantees.

06 · Arcade, social and media spaces

Place an activity. Let the room participate.

Shared arcade library

30 games in one cabinet

The Commons Arcade offers a menu of card, puzzle and retro games. Server-controlled rounds and shared screens let nearby users watch the active game. Maps without an arcade can receive a default cabinet.

Supported multiplayer modes

Ready up or join in

Noughts & Crosses, Four in a Row and Pong support versus play. Selected puzzles support cooperative play, including joining a running cooperative round. Other games retain solo control and spectators; private card hands are not automatically converted to two-player games.

Interactive devices

27 device profiles

Dedicated cabinets sit alongside ATMs, vending terminals, jukeboxes, boomboxes, video players, projectors, photo displays and noticeboards. Market terminals open existing purchasing flows; a decorative machine mesh alone does not process a purchase.

Media and communication

Watch, listen and speak

Media devices coordinate queues, playback controls and timestamps on the server. Browsers fetch and decode supported media. Voice and live camera features use browser capture and peer connections; room signaling and access checks coordinate participation.

Microphone indicators and audio-reactive bars show voice readiness and detected audio energy. Above-avatar speech captions depend on browser speech-recognition support and microphone permission. Playback, camera capture and peer connectivity also depend on the device, network and user’s media permissions; they are not universal server-rendered video streams.

Supported media: the shared queue accepts supported direct media URLs and uploaded files. An arbitrary video webpage is not automatically a playable source. Shared controls synchronize a room’s media state; the server does not guarantee identical decoding or latency on every viewer’s device.

07 · Economy and rewards

Clear balances. Clear rewards. Clear ownership.

The platform uses several distinct accounting systems. MZK in the application is recorded in the server’s user ledger. Room score and arcade practice credits have different purposes and are not interchangeable wallet balances. Supported crypto payments are independently verified before the associated order is delivered.

MZK balance

Used by supported purchases, land and configured game actions. Accepted coin collections can credit this balance through the server reward path.

Room score

Tracks supported activity and quest outcomes. The summer camp quest awards 35 room score; that result does not itself grant 35 MZK.

Arcade practice credits

Cabinet play balances for slots, poker and other supported games. Practice results do not imply a withdrawable prize or a wallet payment.

Other builder counters

Legacy Game Space flows may display Builder Coin or other counters. Their labels and quoted charges belong to that flow, not automatically to the MZK ledger.

08 · Identity, Backpack and ownership

One account record connects what you own and where you play.

  1. AcquireVerified purchase, grant, claim or permitted transfer
  2. BackpackRecord the item under its current owner
  3. ConfigureEquip, reveal, customize or place when supported
  4. UseApply the destination’s access and gameplay rules
  5. TradeTransfer eligible items through an authorized listing
Current account model

Server-backed ownership

Inventory and linked wallet identity are durable application records. Ethereum sign-in uses a challenge and signature; an address alone is not proof of control. A connected wallet does not make all inventory an on-chain asset.

Portable gameplay files

Share references, retain ownership checks.

Eligible owned-item exports include configuration and model URLs; map packages can reference saved scenes and stable room IDs. Import reopens the canonical record under its current permissions. Editing a file cannot grant ownership or recreate a deleted server balance.

Local drafts, preview images and cached balances help the interface, but the server decides current item ownership, listing availability, paid downloads and access to private assets. Free premade builder tools and licensed marketplace items can coexist without equating a sandbox placement with a paid entitlement.

09 · Creator products and retail

A shared creative identity, with explicit product terms.

Digital creation

Models, drawings and assets

Creators can develop supported custom objects, reusable configurations, avatar looks and published environments. Public distribution and paid-model customization follow the applicable account, ownership and upload rules.

Physical products

Merch and character drops

The designer and storefront connect character artwork with product templates, colors, text and saved designs. Checkout, manufacturing and shipping are distinct from gameplay; only an offer’s explicit digital grants enter inventory.

Collection experiences

3D and AR viewing

Compatible models support 3D previews and available AR paths such as WebXR, Scene Viewer or Quick Look. Camera access, an appropriate device and the model’s supported format determine availability.

Entry offers

Loadouts and bundles

Verified offers can grant starter items, land entitlements or MZK. The offer and server receipt define what is included. A product image, rarity label or preview does not establish physical stock, a transferable token or extra game powers.

10 · Persistence, recovery and trust

Keep the world’s records separate from its presentation.

Persistent services

The Node service manages accounts, inventories, purchases, maps and shared-room records. Production persistence depends on configured durable storage for the data and upload directories. Static hosting alone does not supply these services.

Admin master state

Authenticated administrator tools can compile member and land data and export a master state file. Validation and controlled restore can replace server records and included local uploads, with an automatic recovery copy before replacement.

Recovery boundaries

Master files include captured server data and local assets, not deployment secrets, server code or externally hosted files. Blockchain payments are history and are never replayed. Browser-only drafts and unsaved tournaments are outside that capture.

Current protections

Authentication, permission checks, request validation, selected rate limits, private-asset controls, origin checks and HTTP security headers protect supported operations. These measures do not constitute independent certification or a blanket search-engine safety endorsement.

Operational status: backup and master-state functionality exist in the code. Offsite retention, restore drills, production monitoring and load capacity must be operated and measured for the actual deployment; this review does not certify those operational practices.

11 · Development priorities

Make the existing experience more dependable, then expand it.

These are proposed next priorities based on the current implementation. They do not announce completion dates, a token launch or newly purchased entitlements.

Priority 01

Measure movement and rendering

Run repeatable walk, jump, shooting and collision journeys on physical mobile and desktop devices, with concurrent players and increasing scene complexity.

Completion evidence

Published device profiles, frame-time and latency measurements, room-size limits and regression results.

Priority 02

Strengthen map publication and travel

Exercise saved scenes, moved modules, deed transfers, portal round trips and missing-asset recovery across the destination directory.

Completion evidence

Automated map entry and save/reload coverage, clear error recovery and documented persistence behavior.

Priority 03

Deepen activities and social play

Refine arcade onboarding, cooperative join flows, shared media recovery and more varied quests. Expand only the modes with explicit rules and server validation.

Completion evidence

Two-user tests for each supported mode, reconnect handling and a clear feature matrix for unsupported modes.

Priority 04

Improve creator trust and operations

Extend asset diagnostics, moderation, accessibility, rig compatibility and recovery tooling. Document any future economy or interoperability changes before introducing them.

Completion evidence

Versioned validation rules, accessible creator flows, restore exercises and auditable permission boundaries.

12 · Avatar reference standard

Separate the character, the owned instance and the session.

Character definition

The reusable creative identity

Lore, licensed media, compatible rig and animation profiles, fit families and descriptive traits define a character. Different owned instances can refer to that definition.

Avatar instance

A versioned, identifiable record

The avatar schema describes identity, owner reference, media URLs, classes, traits, equipment slots and world permissions. Live health, quest status, position and cooldowns belong in game state.

13 · Asset functions and interoperability

Declare the function. Validate the supported use.

Avatars & wearables

Equip or customize compatible characters and fitted pieces; preserve per-instance appearance settings.

Buildings & scenery

Place geometry, furnish interiors and retain suitable floor and collision structure.

Interactive items

Configure supported behaviors, prompts, distance limits, cooldowns and device profiles.

Vehicles

Use configured drive or flight behavior where the model, scene and game mode support it.

Media & graphics

Display supported content in machines, on props or within permitted creator and product flows.

Land & maps

Resolve a destination, apply ownership rules and retain published scene configuration.

Current validation and access checks are implemented at specific upload, catalog, purchase and gameplay boundaries. A uniform asset certification pipeline—covering geometry budgets, retargeting, rights review, unsafe-content screening and approval across every import path—is a target standard that requires further implementation and operational review.
