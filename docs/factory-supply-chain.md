# Factory production, certification and freight

The missile factory, Atlas Proving Grounds, Mountain Nuclear Research Compound
and Shipping Academy share one saved model, MZK wallet and freight record.
These fictional game collectibles use server-verified production, tests,
certificates, reservations and exchanges.

## Produce, test and certify

1. Fund an order at factory procurement. Operate fabrication, assembly and
   calibration, haul the output with the existing truck and collect it at
   dispatch. Models bought in the MZK market enter the same testing loop.
2. Visit the proving grounds, configure that exact owned model, deploy it at
   the shared pad, then arm and launch from a control desk or VR dashboard.
3. After a qualifying official result, visit **Official model certification**.
   Open the **Production & freight** SVG shelf control and certify the model.
   Its certificate records the score and original model receipt. Older results
   without the model's proof reference require a fresh test.
4. Choose trade or delivery. The board shows the next station, selected model,
   certificate, active manifest and completed shipment history.

Certification follows the exact model through genuine MZK trades. Mountain
access and containment clearance remain personal achievements: buying a
certified model does not copy the seller's facility access.

## Trade finished production

Use **MZK trade** on the freight board to list or unlist an available model at
your chosen price. The existing player marketplace handles the purchase and
MZK transfer atomically. Public listings show verified certification. The buyer
can test, launch or dispatch the same model, including another player's output.
Crafting provenance persists after old trades age out of the public activity feed.

A model reserved for a live test or sealed shipment cannot be sold, deployed as
a display copy, or started in another test. Unlist before packing. Completing,
canceling or recovering its assignment makes it available again.

## Deliver between maps

At **Factory export & market desk**, select a certified model and book:

- **Proving Grounds Supply Depot**: confirm delivery at the range receiving apron.
- **Mountain Facility Supply Hall**: earn both official test clearances, enter
  through the hatch and deliver at the new east service hall.
- **Atlas Export · Jebel Ali to Fujairah**: stage the model at Jebel Ali's
  sealed-model export desk, then complete its actual sea voyage.

Local receipt requires the correct station, on-foot proximity, elapsed travel
time and earned facility clearance where applicable. The reusable model remains
in Backpack; delivery records handling without making a second copy or awarding
extra MZK. Cancel an unshipped booking at the factory or Jebel Ali desk. Deep
bunker access still requires the existing containment puzzle.

## Ship exports and bring parts back

After staging, board a cargo vessel and accept **Atlas Export**. Its four
balanced units fit the starter feeder. Load, secure, depart, follow the actual
checkpoints, berth at Fujairah and unload. Only that linked voyage's verified
settlement completes the model's manifest. Normal voyage MZK and stock rewards
share the existing daily shipping cap.

Leaving the voyage, world travel, cancellation or losing the connection returns
the sealed model to Jebel Ali for collection. It stays reserved until collected.
Reloading or retrying does not duplicate the model or reward.

At a shipping port, exchange one **Delivered machine parts** stock unit for one
**20 MZK production credit**. The next valid factory order consumes one credit
and subtracts 20 MZK from its normal cost. Credits and stock stay in canonical
account storage; failed orders and duplicate requests do not spend another
credit. This connects shipping rewards back to production and market exports.

## Maps, Builder and VR

The factory adds an export warehouse and freight counter; the range adds
certification and receiving aprons; the compound adds an accessible service hall;
shipping ports add Atlas export and receiving desks. Existing driving routes,
launch stations, return links and compound locks remain usable.

Three reusable assets are available in MZK markets and Map Builder:

| Item | MZK |
| --- | ---: |
| Factory Export Terminal | 240 |
| Factory Certification Kiosk | 180 |
| Factory Freight Crate | 70 |

Copied maps retain editable terminal roles and approach points when moved,
rotated or removed. Official certificates and sea exports still require their
canonical destinations. Custom maps cannot issue official clearance.

VR uses the native headset menu for model and destination selection,
certification, listing, staging, delivery, cancellation, returned cargo and stock
exchange. Controller actions call the same server commands. Desktop and touch
use the responsive SVG shelf dialog. Existing launch dashboards continue to
show shared countdowns, telemetry and compound controls.

## Verification

Run factory, logistics, shipping, market and authoritative-room tests together.
Browser checks cover the freight board, live range controls and emulated XR
events. Run `npm run build && npm run check` for build validation.
