# Skyline construction operations

The shared yard is 336 × 304 m, with 13 real earthwork areas. The Construction map and its editable Builder copy carry the same terrain, signs and haul roads. Open **Crew / jobs** for the site operations plan, locations, machine instructions and live progress. The compact Work / Drive controls stay unchanged.

| Work area | Site X / Z | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pavement reclamation | 198 / 12 | Rip the flat 24 cm pavement using the bucket or blade. Reclaimed pavement becomes gravel cargo. |
| New road | 198 / 92 | Tip gravel or reclaimed aggregate and blade it into a level 20 cm road base. |
| Utility trench | 126 / -44 | Excavate a 64 m utility trench to 3 m depth, loading from the shoulders. |
| Basement | 76 / -40 | Excavate successive reachable lifts to 6 m depth. |
| Recycled aggregate receiving | 198 / 166 | Tip, stockpile and reload aggregate. The stockpile objective is 30 m³. |
| Earth platform | 94 / 192 | Haul, tip, spread and level 30 cm of soil or gravel. |
| Skyscraper steel | 5 / -10 | Lift and secure steel; four beams complete each of 16 floors. |

Existing starter excavation, spoil receiving, deep soil, gravel extraction, soil and gravel stockpiles and fill platform remain available. Extended haul lanes connect the new works to the original loading approach and the south gate. Raised survey markers and signs sit outside working cells; static road slabs never cover excavatable ground.

Use **Work** for hydraulic levers and assisted cycles. Use **Drive** for wheels/tracks. Raise and curl the bucket before travelling. Park the truck with its body lowered within the excavator loading envelope to receive a bucket. Drive to a receiving patch and tip with the rear discharge clear. Trucks can also backfill excavations. A lowered dozer blade moves existing material; adjust its height for the desired finished grade. Park cancels a work cycle and neutralizes travel and hydraulics.

Progress measures authoritative terrain, material and secured steel. A heap does not complete a road: each cell needs aggregate coverage and the correct level. Excavation cannot go below its authored bottom, off-patch dumping retains cargo, and material transfer conserves volume. Asphalt is a dark surface layer in the existing gravel ledger, so it can be recycled without inventing material. Saved rooms append missing work areas once, preserving worked cells, payloads and existing counters.

Validation: construction regression tests; a real-collision excavator → truck → road material-chain test; finite pavement, visible soil exposure, graded-road objectives and saved-site expansion tests; mobile machine/dashboard and site-plan browser checks.
