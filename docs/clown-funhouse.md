# Midnight Laughs Funhouse

Open **Explorer options → Midnight Laughs Funhouse**, choose it in the world
directory, or visit
`model-explorer.html?environment=muzikaz-clown-funhouse&house=muzikaz-clown-funhouse&mode=multiplayer&autoplay=1`.

This permanent 120-metre shared destination adds a spooky clown carnival to the
existing game. Walk through the giant grinning doorway into the horror house,
explore the crooked mirror maze, find the monster ball pit and wind up the
surprise box. On the midway, board the clown car or dizzy spinner and play their
timing games. Striped carnival stalls, balloons, clown hosts and the
water-gun gallery complete the grounds. Lantern walks, theatrical clockwork,
story displays, ornate house framing and clearer wayfinding add places to
explore between games.

Every funhouse performer, resident and visiting player appears as a creepy clown,
with pale makeup, dark eyes and a toothy grin. This appearance follows the
funhouse and its Builder copies. Your saved avatar selection stays intact and
returns when you leave for another world.

Use the normal **WASD / arrows** or touch controls to walk. **View / V** switches
camera views. Approach an attraction and press **E / Use** to open its controls.
Choose an attraction's first-person play button to close the menu and play in
the world. Its compact action bar shows the timer, score and current cue. Expand
**Actions** to reveal touch buttons and help; keyboard actions keep working
while it is collapsed. Look freely around either ride using normal look controls.
**Escape / Stop** ends the current activity; rides also have an **Exit ride**
button. Walking resumes after leaving a seat. The local map marks each area.

| Experience | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| Water-gun gallery | Aim at the glowing clown in the actual gallery and use **Shoot**, click/tap it, or use **1–6**. A round lasts 30 seconds; each hit earns 10 points. The menu also retains its large target buttons. |
| Clown car | Board the seated 20-second ride and use **H / Honk** when the horn cue lights up. Each successful cue earns 10 points. |
| Dizzy spinner | Board for 15 seconds and balance toward the lit side with **1 / Left** or **2 / Right**. Each correct cue earns 10 points. Use **Gentle motion** to adjust the ride. |
| Surprise box | Wind three times with **F / Wind**, then use **F / Catch** when the clown appears. A successful catch earns 50 points. |
| Monster ball pit | Play a 20-second round: aim at the glowing hoop and shoot, or use the four **Toss** buttons / **1–4**. Each correct toss earns 10 points. |
| Mirror maze | Start at the house lobby, walk through the ordered checkpoint markers and ring the physical exit bell in the far corner within two minutes. **F / Check in** checks the next marker. |
| Dressing room | Try the spinning propeller hat and oversized clown shoes. |

Shots, throws and winding have short refill intervals. Scores, cue timing and
maze progress are checked by the shared room. Three gallery hits earn its stamp.
Complete the car, spinner, surprise box and maze, and wear both costume pieces
to fill all six passport stamps. Best gallery scores and costume choices are
saved with your player in this map's room. Trying the dressing-room
costumes does not grant an owned Backpack item; permanent copies are sold in
the market.

## The Lost Laugh Trail

Begin the optional ten-minute adventure at the welcome trail marker. Follow
six clues across the midway, wardrobe, water gallery, spinner, monster playroom
and mirror maze. Walk to each marked object and inspect it in order to uncover
its story and a theatrical surprise. The trail shows the next hint and your
progress. Expand **Trail** for the hint, inspection and Stop buttons, or use
**F** beside the next clue. Stop ends the adventure whenever you want.

Each discovery earns 25 points. Finding all six completes the trail and adds a
time bonus of up to 100 points, with a personal best saved in this shared room.
You can enjoy attractions between discoveries. Using map travel or leaving the
world ends the active trail. This adventure belongs to the permanent funhouse;
individual placed attractions retain their own games and rides.

## Night Watch

Enter the grinning doorway and approach the watch lantern in the house lobby
to start **Night Watch**. You have three minutes to seal four haunted lanterns:
the nursery, clockwork theatre, crooked mirrors and watching portraits. Follow
the current hint, walk close to the glowing lantern, and use **F / Seal lantern**
or aim at its physical seal control. Each seal wakes a theatrical shadow or
clown reveal. The fourth seal ends the watch with the final reveal.

Each lantern earns 35 points toward the round. Completing all four awards those
140 points plus a time bonus of up to 60; stopping or running out of time does
not award a partial round. Your best completed watch is saved in this shared
room. The Lost Laugh Trail can remain active while you play. Starting another
carnival game ends the watch, and you can stop at any time from its controls.

In VR, either controller can select the actual lantern controls, or use the
Night Watch page on the floating board for the next hint, seal and Stop actions.
The haunted house adds watching faces, shadow figures and eerie sound cues
without changing your view direction. Reduced motion keeps decorative movement
still while retaining the lantern cues and reveals. Existing voice, effects and
master mute settings continue to apply.

In a WebXR headset, either controller trigger selects physical attraction
controls and targets. **X / B** opens the game menu, which includes the nearby
attraction actions and a floating control board. The board is part of the 3D
scene and can be hidden or recentered from that menu. The headset keeps its
tracked view during seated rides. Ending the session or changing worlds releases
held controls and closes its attraction board.

The floating board recenters after boarding or leaving a ride. Its action pages
keep every target and stop control reachable, and its trail view carries
adventure hints and inspection controls without covering the entire scene.

The VR game menu also carries the available actions and settings from existing
game panels. It updates as rounds and selections change, so those controls stay
usable inside the headset.

Attractions use the existing shared-room connection. The destination disables
ambient RAD-TOX encounters and does not charge or award MZK for carnival
activities. Regular world travel remains available from the toolbar.

## Clown voices, sounds and music

The carnival has 30 original voiced clown lines, with matching captions. The
hosts welcome visitors, tease missed shots, celebrate catches and discoveries,
and offer suspiciously cheerful advice. Honks, squeaky shoes, winding cranks,
water splashes, ball tosses, bells and short scare stings accompany the real
attraction actions.

The clown car plays **Midnight Motor Waltz**, an original minor-key carnival
tune with calliope-like melody and a swaying bass. Nearby attraction sounds use
the existing spatial audio setting, so their direction and volume follow your
position in first person and VR.

Use **Clown audio settings** at the lower right, or expand **Clown voices,
sounds & music** in an attraction menu. Voices, music and effects each have a
mute button and volume control. You can replay the last clown line or preview
the car tune. Player settings still control all audio, NPC conversations and
game effects; a funhouse toggle does not override those mutes.

In VR, the same settings, replay and preview actions appear in the floating
board and the game menu. The latest clown caption appears on that board. Audio
starts after a game gesture; leaving the world stops its music and sound cues.

## Map list, market and Builder

**Midnight Laughs Funhouse** appears in the world list, the **Clown funhouse**
world filter, the Game Market and the Builder Market. Its map listing includes
**Visit map**, **Edit map template**, and an itemized component list. Each
**View item** button opens that component's exact market offer.

The **Clown funhouse** item category contains 28 individually purchasable native
models: the horror house, mirror partition and exit bell; water gallery, toy
water blaster and clown target; clown car, dizzy spinner, dressing room,
propeller hat and giant shoes; ball pit and fright box; popcorn, ticket and candy
stalls; circus tent, fence, balloons, bunting, lamp, welcome board and midway
foundation; and five playable clown characters—ringmaster, juggler, greeter,
driver and scare clown. Characters also work as placed NPCs with their own
dialogue. Placed attraction copies retain their action menus, games and rides.

Purchases use the existing MZK wallet and deliver once to the account's Backpack.
Owned native copies retain their functions and can be reused without a per-use
MZK charge. Use the avatar chooser for a purchased clown character, equip the
hat and shoes from Backpack, or place owned props and attractions in a world.
Preview rotation and zoom only inspect the item; they do not buy or grant it.

The editable template turns the carnival assemblies into separate movable
objects. The component list also identifies costumes, targets and characters
already contained inside complete attractions; the template avoids placing
those pieces twice. Its fixed walkable foundation remains part of the map so removing a
placed prop cannot remove the player's underlying floor. Buying components or
opening the template does not grant ownership of the public map or a land deed.
