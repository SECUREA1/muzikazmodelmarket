# MUZIKAZ Fitness Centre

Enter **MUZIKAZ Fitness Centre** from the world map's **Fitness** category or the Explorer options menu. The permanent destination is `muzikaz-fitness-centre`:

`model-explorer.html?environment=muzikaz-fitness-centre&house=muzikaz-fitness-centre&mode=multiplayer&autoplay=1`

Walk with WASD / arrow keys or the touch controls. Use **V** to switch between first person and top down. The connected local map shows training zones, people, arrival and return gateways. Open the **Gym** control for the station directory and floor plan. Walk to a station and press **E / Use**, or select its training action in the directory once nearby.

The 120 × 80 metre gym has eleven playable activities. The original seven training stations remain available:

- Boxing: follow the punch and defence combination.
- Bench press: control each repetition with precise timing.
- Deadlift: match the lift and lockout cues.
- Agility: react to directional cues.
- Jump rope: keep the rhythm and build a streak.
- Rowing: alternate drive and recovery.
- Balance: make precise stability corrections.

## Competitive sports

The championship boxing ring now has four ropes, padded red and blue corners, open entry steps, an overhead scoreclock and lighting, judges and ringside seating. At its south entrance, choose **Championship Boxing · AI Sparring**, select a rival and start a three-round bout. Time jabs and crosses to score, then guard or slip the announced counterattack. Each round has four judged exchanges. Use the labelled keys or touch buttons in the training panel.

Enter the competition wing through either opening in the east partition:

- **Basketball shootout:** prepare each of four shots with left/right aim cues, then release. Shot preparation and release accuracy both affect the basket.
- **Table tennis:** return the ball with forehand, backhand and smash cues. Rally outcomes decide the match score.
- **Sprint and hurdles:** alternate left/right drive cues and clear the hurdle at each split. Stride accuracy and hurdle penalties determine race distance.

Nine named AI rivals have different ratings and styles. Choose an eligible opponent before a match; the server creates the cue sequence, judges accepted inputs and determines the result. The sport scorecard is separate from the 1,200-point precision score used for fitness rewards. Completed matches save win/draw/loss records with the normal account progress. There is no wager or loss of wallet funds.

Each round explains its keys and offers touch buttons. Scores come from the room server's clock and accepted inputs. Finishing a qualifying round records a durable MZK receipt and fitness progress in the existing wallet database. Repeated settlement requests reuse the receipt. Rewards have a daily limit; rounds remain playable after the limit. Cancelled or invalid rounds award nothing. No entry stake is charged.

The gym has **18 AI characters**. **Maya Chen** coaches boxing and movement; **Leon Brooks** coaches strength and conditioning. Approach either trainer for technique tips, rival information and feedback on your actual match results. Referee **Nia Bennett** officiates the ring, **Darius** and **Tessa** demonstrate scored sparring, and members train, recover, spectate and move along clear paths. Basketball, table-tennis and sprint partners have their own animations and routines. AI demonstrations award no player currency. The gym disables ambient combat, traffic and random reward pickups.

Fitness content uses the existing permanent world directory, atlas links, multiplayer room, procedural collision geometry, player controls and wallet service. It requires the Node server, as do the other authoritative games; static hosting alone does not settle rewards.

## Gold map and Builder upgrades

The gym has a gold entry in the world map list, atlas and destination wheel. In **Builder**, choose **Gold Fitness Centre** from the template selector or the gold world shortcut. The gym is copied into your own draft with individually editable equipment; changes do not modify the permanent public gym.

Open **ITEMS → Fitness & gym** for all **31 equipment and furnishing types**. The expanded template contains **65 editable assemblies**, including the advanced ring, a boxing scoreboard, basketball hoop, table-tennis table, sprint gates and ringside seating. Select an item to move, rotate, scale, recolour, duplicate or remove it with the existing Builder controls. Training prompts follow edited station equipment, and removing a station removes its challenge from that draft. Existing saved custom gyms keep moved, disabled and deleted items; use a fresh template or add the new upgrades to expand an older draft.

In the game marketplace, open **All game items → Fitness & gym upgrades** to buy individual equipment upgrades with MZK. Purchased items appear in your existing Backpack for placement. Prices and ownership use the canonical wallet service, and training equipment keeps its activity settings when placed into a fitness map.
