ACCOUNT & APP SAFETY

Security is ongoing work.

Use the official HTTPS site and keep your browser updated. A website or installed web app cannot promise complete security or guarantee approval from Google or another browser provider.

Report a security issue

Contact jodelchad@chaines.io privately with the affected URL, reproduction steps and impact. Do not send passwords, session tokens, recovery phrases, private keys or other members' data.

Responsible disclosure helps us investigate before an issue is shared publicly. This page does not promise a response deadline or bounty.

Protect your member account and wallet

  • Choose a unique member password and sign out on shared devices.
  • Never enter a wallet recovery phrase or private key into a website, support chat or email.
  • Read the destination, amount and network in your wallet before approving a transaction. A wallet sign-in signature and a payment are different actions.
  • Only download files you intentionally requested and trust. User-created content and external links are not independently certified as malware-free.

Browser permissions

Camera, microphone and location access remain controlled by your browser. Review permission requests and turn off access you no longer need in your browser's site settings. Shared gameplay and media can be visible or audible to other participants.

Google and browser warnings

If your browser warns about a page or download, follow the warning. Report the exact URL and warning text through the security contact above. Safe Browsing classifications, search listings and reviews are managed by Google, not by this page.
