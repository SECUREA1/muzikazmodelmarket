Controls and match rules

Skate with WASD/arrows. Aim with the pointer, Space shoots, E passes, F poke-checks, Q body-checks, and Shift lofts a shot. X switches skaters, L changes your shift at a stoppage or near the bench, G pulls/returns the goalie, and T takes your team’s timeout during a stoppage. H hooks, J slashes and K holds: referees can penalize illegal contact.

Rookie is the relaxed tier. Club, Pro and Elite increase AI pressure. Delayed offside requires attackers to tag up; hybrid icing uses the race to the end-zone dots. Minor and major penalties affect manpower. Tied exhibition games use 3-on-3 overtime then a shootout; cups use playoff sudden death.
